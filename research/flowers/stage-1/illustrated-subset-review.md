# Illustrated 36-entry subset review

Open [`illustrated-subset-review.html`](./illustrated-subset-review.html) beside the `images/` directory, or use the portable single-file [`illustrated-subset-review-standalone.html`](./illustrated-subset-review-standalone.html) when copying the report elsewhere. Both contain the full visual gallery, Turkish story summaries, source links, confidence notes, filters, and the proposed 10-entry coherent starter set. The row-level review data is also available in [`illustrated-subset-review.csv`](./illustrated-subset-review.csv).

## Verified comparison

| Measure | Full set | Illustrated subset |
|---|---:|---:|
| Entries | 331 | 36 (10.9%) |
| Entries present in multiple sources | 124 (37,5%) | 27 (75,0%) |
| Entries present in all three sources | 41 (12,4%) | 12 (33,3%) |
| Median context length | 251 characters | 393 characters |

The illustrated subset is narrower in botanical/form coverage but stronger than a random tenth of the corpus: it disproportionately retains cross-source, culturally recurring flowers and has longer source context. Its main weakness is visual mixing across three books. The proposed 10-entry starter set uses exact, target-dominant Tyas illustrations to create one visual family without entering application or data-model work.
